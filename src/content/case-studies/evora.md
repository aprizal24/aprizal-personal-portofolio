  <div class="cs-head">
    <p class="cs-label">Problem → Solution</p>
    <div class="cs-head__main">
      <h2 class="cs-title">Research</h2>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-ps">
    <div class="cs-ps__item">
      <p class="cs-label">The problem</p>
      <p class="cs-ps__text">People often discover events through friends and social media, but important information can be scattered across different sources. Details such as performers, date, exact location, ticket prices, and discounts can be difficult to find, making the decision to attend less clear.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="cs-ps__item cs-ps__item--solution">
      <p class="cs-label">The solution</p>
      <p class="cs-ps__text">Evora brings event discovery and ticket purchasing into one structured experience, helping users explore events, understand the details that matter, compare ticket options, and complete their purchase with greater confidence.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
  <div class="cs-head cs-head--sub">
    <p class="cs-label">Persona</p>
    <div class="cs-head__main">
      <h3 class="cs-subtitle">Meet the primary user</h3>
      <p class="cs-text">The primary user represents someone who discovers events through social media or recommendations and wants enough information to decide quickly whether an event is worth attending.</p>
